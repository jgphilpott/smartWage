Don't Have a Contract Address Yet?

Employee contracts on smartWage are created by your employer. Once your employer registers you (using your wallet address), a dedicated EmployeePortal contract is automatically deployed for you.

Ask your employer for your contract address — it's shown next to your name in their "Active Employees" table — and paste it above to connect.

The contract source is at contracts/employee/EmployeePortal.sol in this repository.
